# Video description template

Paste into Studio › Settings › Upload defaults › Description. Replace the `{…}` parts per video.
The first two lines show in search — put the main keyword there.

```
{ONE-LINE HOOK WITH THE MAIN KEYWORD, e.g. "GTA 6 gameplay — the first hour in Vice City, every mission explained."}

🔔 Subscribe for daily GTA 6: https://www.youtube.com/@SixStarHeat?sub_confirmation=1
🔴 Live every Fri · Sat · Sun: https://www.youtube.com/@SixStarHeat/streams

⏱️ Chapters

{0:45 …}
{…}

• GTA 6 Full Walkthrough: {playlist link}
• GTA 6 Guides & Secrets: {playlist link}

🎮 Played on: {PS5 / PS5 Pro / Xbox Series X}

Six Star Heat is a fan channel, not affiliated with Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.

#GTA6 #GTAVI #{SECOND KEYWORD WITHOUT SPACES}
```

## Tags (≤ 15 per video)
Always: `GTA 6`, `GTA VI`, `Grand Theft Auto 6`, `GTA 6 gameplay`, `Six Star Heat`
Then 5–10 specific to the video (mission name, location, car, "GTA 6 money guide", etc.).

## Title rules
- Main keyword first: **"GTA 6 …"**. Under 60 characters so it is not cut on mobile.
- One emotional hook: *INSANE, Secret, Nobody noticed, Before you play, I tried…*
- Episode numbers only on walkthrough videos: `GTA 6 Walkthrough Part 3 – {Mission} (PS5)`.
