# Live streaming setup

## Before the first stream
1. Verify the phone number in YouTube (Settings › Channel › Feature eligibility). Live is enabled **up to 24 h** later.
2. Studio › Create › **Go live** › *Stream* (encoder). Copy the **stream key** into OBS (or use the PS5/Xbox built-in YouTube broadcasting).
3. Default stream settings: Category Gaming · Game *Grand Theft Auto VI* · Not made for kids ·
   Live chat on · Slow mode 5 s · Live DVR on · Auto-start/auto-stop on.

## Console-only (no PC)
- **PS5:** Create button › Broadcast › YouTube › 1080p60 (or 4K if your upload is > 25 Mbps).
- **Xbox:** use a capture card + OBS, or the Twitch/YouTube app route.

## OBS (PC + capture card) recommended settings
| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Output | 1920×1080 @ 60 fps |
| Encoder | NVENC H.264 / x264 *veryfast* |
| Bitrate | 9,000–12,000 Kbps (needs ~15 Mbps upload) |
| Keyframe interval | 2 s |
| Audio | 160 Kbps AAC · game audio + mic on separate tracks |

Scenes: `Starting Soon` (banner + countdown) · `Gameplay` (watermark star bottom-right) · `BRB` · `Ending` (subscribe + next stream time).

## Titles (keyword first, < 70 chars)
- `🔴 GTA 6 LIVE — First Hours Gameplay (Launch Night)`
- `🔴 GTA 6 LIVE — Story Walkthrough Part {N} (PS5)`
- `🔴 GTA 6 LIVE — 5-Star Wanted Chaos With Chat`
- `🔴 GTA 6 LIVE — Hunting Secrets & Easter Eggs`

## Schedule
Fri · Sat · Sun, same time every week (pick the slot and keep it; e.g. 20:00 CET / 2 pm ET hits Europe + Americas).
Schedule each stream 24–48 h before in Studio so the "Upcoming" card and notifications go out.

## During and after
- Pin a chat message: "Subscribe + 👍 = next mission vote".
- Turn in-game radio OFF (music Content ID claims).
- After the stream: cut 3–5 Shorts and 1 highlight video from the VOD; add VOD to *GTA 6 Live Streams* playlist.
